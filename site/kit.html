A FLEXIBLE STARTING POINT

Build around real needs.

Ireland’s Be Emergency Ready booklet recommends a 72-hour supply of non-perishable food, drinking water, medicines, lighting and warmth. What is suitable varies by household.

  • Use things you already own.
  • Check dates and rotate supplies.
  • Plan for medicines, infants, disability and pets.
  • Follow current official advice in an emergency.
