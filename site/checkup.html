A PRACTICAL STARTING POINT

Why begin with 72 hours?

It gives a household a clear, manageable first planning target. It is not a prediction of how long a disruption will last or a guarantee that every emergency is resolved within three days.

72HOURS
IRELAND’S GUIDANCE

Start with the essentials you would use.

The Be Emergency Ready booklet recommends keeping non-perishable food for 72 hours and 2–3 litres of drinking water per person per day, alongside medicines, lighting, warmth and household-specific needs.

EU PREPAREDNESS

Plan for the initial period when systems may be under pressure.

European Commission population-preparedness guidance uses at least 72 hours as a self-sufficiency baseline, while response capacity and supply arrangements are mobilised.

Read the EU guidance ↗
MAKE IT YOURS

Adapt the baseline to the people in your home.

Households may need more or different supplies depending on health, mobility, children, pets, local risks and official instructions. Start with what is affordable and useful to you.
