ABOUT READY72

Preparedness information,
made easier to use.

READY72 is an independent learning prototype exploring how official Irish information, big data and AI could help households understand disruptions and prepare practical next steps.

WHY THIS IDEA

From a confusing booklet to a clearer household view.

The idea grew from seeing how emergency-preparedness booklets can feel unclear or worrying, especially for older relatives. READY72 aims to explain what sources are reporting, why an update could matter, and what a household can do at its own pace. It does not predict emergencies or replace official advice.

TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Where the information comes from

Live feeds, public listings, historical datasets and general advice are labelled separately. A missing feed is shown as unavailable, never as an all-clear.

Floods & rivers

Recent OPW gauge readings are observations, not flood thresholds or official warnings. The rainfall study uses Met Éireann gridded estimates from 2016–2025.

03

Electricity

READY72 reads a public-facing ESB PowerCheck data route as a work in progress. Coverage, continuity and reuse conditions are not confirmed; reported records may overlap and are not household-level status.

ESB PowerCheck ↗WIP PUBLIC FEED
04

Water supply

Public Uisce Éireann service-map listings are matched by county where possible. The records may be incomplete and do not confirm the status of an individual address.

Uisce Éireann updates ↗PUBLIC MAP LISTINGS
05

Cyber security

National Cyber Security Centre advisories provide security context. An advisory is not proof that a particular household or Irish infrastructure service has been attacked.

Irish NCSC updates ↗OFFICIAL ADVISORIES
06

Essential services

For fire risk and transport, READY72 links to official information from Teagasc/DAFM and TII. These services are not currently presented as integrated live data feeds.

Teagasc fire risk ↗TII traffic information ↗OFFICIAL LINKS · NOT LIVE FEEDS
HOW TO READ A READY72 SIGNAL

Freshness and limits belong beside the finding.

Current

What the source most recently returned, with its check time and status.

Evidence

Historical data and documented events, with the time period and limits stated.

Prepare

Practical steps drawn from guidance and adapted to household needs.

UPDATE INFORMATION

A check time is not a guarantee of real-time coverage.

READY72 checks integrated sources when the page loads or a user refreshes it; some results may be briefly cached and each provider updates on its own schedule. The displayed time describes the READY72 check, while a source’s observation or notice time is shown separately when available. READY72 does not send push alerts. For official warnings and urgent instructions, use the linked authority directly.

WHAT THIS PROTOTYPE IS NOT

Useful context, with clear limits.

  • READY72 is not a government service, emergency alert authority or emergency response service.
  • Official agencies publish warnings and operational instructions; READY72’s explanations and planning prompts are educational guidance, not official alerts.
  • “No matching record” does not prove that no local issue exists.
  • Public map listings do not verify an individual home’s service status.
  • AI responses can be wrong or incomplete. Follow current official instructions and emergency-service advice.

Immediate danger: call 112 or 999.
