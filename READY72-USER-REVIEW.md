# READY72 quick usability review

## Purpose

Check whether a first-time user can find relevant local information, understand what the sources do and do not say, and take a useful preparedness step. This is a small formative review of a prototype, not a representative study or proof of impact.

## Ask participants to try these tasks

1. Select a county or use the location option. Find the latest weather or water information for that area.
2. Explain in their own words what one displayed item means, who reported it and when READY72 last checked it. Ask whether a river reading is the same thing as a flood warning.
3. Complete the private readiness check and follow one suggested next step to the kit builder or household plan.
4. Ask READY72 one question about a current service update. Ask what they would do if the source were unavailable or showed no matching notice.

Do not ask participants to provide an Eircode, exact address, medical details, account credentials or their private household plan.

## Record without personal identifiers

For each task, note:

- Completed without help / completed with help / not completed.
- Where they paused or misunderstood a label.
- Whether they could identify the source, check time and limits of the information.
- Whether the next action was clear and useful.
- A 1–5 confidence rating after the task, where 1 means “not confident” and 5 means “very confident.”

Record a short anonymous quote only with the participant’s agreement. Do not record names or sensitive household circumstances.

## Questions to ask afterwards

- What would you use READY72 for first?
- Which part felt most useful?
- Was any status or label difficult to understand?
- Did you know what to do after seeing a notice or result?
- What would stop you trusting or returning to the site?

## Report honestly

Summarise the number of people who tried it, the tasks that caused friction, and the changes made afterward. Keep review findings separate from live-source testing and from the self-check score. Do not describe a small convenience sample as representative of Irish households.

## Project and future business framing

READY72 is a prototype for a household preparedness information platform: source-linked local updates, evidence-led explanations, an AI question-answering interface, and practical planning tools. A future shop is a possible business extension, not part of the current product evaluation. Keep this concept separate from measured outcomes until a later version is tested.
