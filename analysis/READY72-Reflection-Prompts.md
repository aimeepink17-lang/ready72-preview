# READY72 reflection prompts

Write the reflection in your own words after checking the detailed assessment brief and your final results. These prompts are not a completed personal reflection.

1. What product decision did you make, and what evidence influenced it?
2. What did the 2016 rainfall-grid discrepancy teach you about checking data before presenting a chart?
3. How did you distinguish current alerts, observations, historical estimates and general advice in the product?
4. What did the chatbot safety checks show? Which tests remain necessary once a model key and deployment environment are available?
5. What user or stakeholder feedback did you actually collect, and how did it change the design?
6. What are the limits of this dataset and the current utility-data integrations?
7. How would you improve the analysis, source coverage or evaluation if you had another month?
8. Which skills did you develop that are relevant to your current or future work?

Do not claim stakeholder testing, production reliability, model accuracy or completed deployment unless you have evidence for it.
